`Textarea` takes a `fill` prop that stretches its container to the parent's block size and makes the `<textarea>` at least as tall as the container. With `grow`, textareas side by side, such as in a `Grid`, keep equal heights without custom CSS, up to the container's maximum block size; raise `maxBlockSize` to lift that limit.
