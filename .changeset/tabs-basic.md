Add `Tabs`, `TabList`, `Tab` and `TabPanel` for in-page tabs. Tabs are matched to panels by a string `value`, the selection is controlled with `value`/`onValueChange` or left uncontrolled with `defaultValue`, and inactive panels stay mounted. Styled by the new `tabs` component theme.
